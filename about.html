I am a data analyst with a background that bridges mathematics, engineering, and the social sciences, and I enjoy applying this mix to solve real problems. My path has taken me from mechanical engineering at the University of Michigan to research in health and behavioral sciences at the University of Colorado Denver, and on to consulting, teaching, and data-focused roles with universities, public sector agencies, and nonprofits. Along the way I’ve developed strong skills in SQL, Python, Power BI, and GIS, and I’ve applied them in projects ranging from global health indicator analysis to species mapping and warehouse sales dashboards. I am motivated by opportunities to learn quickly, translate analysis into clear insights, and build tools that others can use and understand. This site highlights that journey and the skills I bring to future roles.
