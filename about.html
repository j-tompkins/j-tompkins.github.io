I have a background that bridges mathematics, engineering, and the social sciences, and I enjoy applying this mix to solve real problems. My path has taken me from mathematics at Morehouse College, to mechanical engineering at the University of Michigan, later to research in health and behavioral sciences at the University of Colorado Denver In my career, my academic foundation opened doors in consulting, education, and data-focused roles with universities, public sector agencies, and nonprofits. Along the way I have developed strong skills in SQL, Python, Power BI, and GIS, and I have applied them in roles throughout my career and worked to expand them in across multiple projects. I am motivated by opportunities to learn quickly, translate analysis into clear insights, and build tools that others can use and understand. This site highlights that journey and the skills I bring to future roles.

What ties all of these experiences together is a commitment to clear and practical data work. I enjoy building models and dashboards that not only answer technical questions in areas of personal interest, but also to build skills that will lead to usable and straightforward outcomes in my future endeavors. Whether it is cleaning and preparing complex datasets, creating visualizations that highlight key patterns, or experimenting with machine learning and data engineering projects, I approach each step as a chance to grow and to make the results accessible. My portfolio is meant to reflect that ongoing process, showing both where I have been and where I am heading as I expand into more advanced analytics and reporting.
