Healthy Eating Index and Blood Pressure

R • Survey-weighted linear regression • NHANES • Public health

A survey-weighted NHANES analysis examining whether overall diet quality, measured by the Healthy Eating Index, is associated with systolic blood pressure among U.S. adults.

Question: Is higher diet quality associated with lower systolic blood pressure?

Key result: A 10-point higher HEI score was associated with approximately 1.35 mmHg lower systolic blood pressure after adjustment for age, sex, and race/ethnicity.
