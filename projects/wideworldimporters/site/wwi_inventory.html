Inventory Health — Wide World Importers (Power BI)

A practical, single‑page monitor with cards, clustered columns, line trend, pie/treemap, and targeted exception KPIs.

Overview & Driving Question

Question: Where are stock risks and slow‑moving items concentrated, and how do we improve turns without harming service? The final design centers on a clustered column chart for location/category variance, a line trend for context, and targeted KPI cards. A treemap and small pie provide fast composition cues.

I intentionally used a compact layout with clear color rules for risk, and structured the page for rapid triage: KPIs → clusters → trend → composition. Tooltips explain definitions; labels are shown only where they aid action. Filters are simple and consistent to keep the rhythm of review smooth.

Method & Build Notes

  • Model
    • Inventory movement fact with Product, Date, Location dimensions.
    • Cleaned date table used consistently across visuals.
  • Measures
    • Total Sales & Total Quantity
    • Inventory Turns and Days of Supply
    • Stockout Rate and Low‑Velocity Flag
  • Visuals & UX
    • Clustered columns: variance by location/category with conditional data labels.
    • Line chart: trend over time for context and seasonality.
    • Treemap + pie: quick composition and share.
    • Cards: spotlight at‑risk SKUs and key totals.

Findings & Reflection

Risk concentrated in a handful of categories and locations; turns improved by focusing on stale SKUs and right‑sizing thresholds. The clustered columns made outliers visible, while the trend line confirmed whether changes were structural or temporary.

Lessons: fewer visuals, clearer definitions, and just enough interactivity to support daily review. The layout is designed for quick scanning and decisive action.
